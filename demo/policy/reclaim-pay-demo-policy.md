# Reclaim Pay Demo Travel Policy

**Policy code:** RP-DEMO-2026
**Effective date:** 1 November 2026
**Owner:** Human Resources
**Currency:** Malaysian Ringgit (RM / MYR)

> Demo document. This policy belongs to a fictional company and exists only for
> the Reclaim Pay hackathon demo. Do not use it as a real HR policy.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out which business expenses the company reimburses, how much it
reimburses, and which claims can be paid automatically without an HR check.

It applies to every permanent and contract employee who spends their own money on
company business. HR administers the policy. Claims are submitted through Reclaim
with a photo or PDF of the receipt.

## 2. General rules

1. Every claim must be supported by an itemised receipt that shows the merchant,
   the date and the total amount.
2. Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the receipt date. Receipts older than
   90 days are not reimbursed.
3. Each receipt may be claimed once only. A receipt that was already claimed, by
   the same employee or by anyone else, is not reimbursed again.
4. Expenses for the same purchase must not be split across several receipts or
   several claims to stay under an auto-approval amount.
5. Amounts are reimbursed in MYR. Foreign currency receipts are converted at the
   rate on the receipt date.
6. Alcohol, personal entertainment, fines and penalties are not reimbursed.

## 3. Reimbursable expense categories

The company reimburses exactly four expense categories: **Transportation**,
**Meals**, **Accommodations** and **Equipment**.

### Auto-approval amounts

A claim at or below its category's auto-approval amount may be approved and paid
automatically. A claim above it is reviewed by HR.

| Category | Auto-approval amount (RM) |
|---|---|
| Transportation | 100 |
| Meals | 100 |
| Accommodations | 250 |
| Equipment | 300 |

### 3.1 Transportation

Covers ride-hailing, taxis, highway tolls and parking for business travel.

- Auto-approval amount: RM100 per trip.
- The receipt must show the pick-up and drop-off locations or the toll plaza.
- Ride-hailing and taxi trips must be for business, for example to a client site,
  an airport or a company event.

### 3.2 Meals

Covers meals taken while working away from the employee's normal office, such as
on a client visit or a business trip.

- Auto-approval amount: RM100 per meal.
- One receipt covers one meal. Several receipts from the same merchant on the same
  day are treated as one meal for the RM100 limit.
- Meals above RM100 are reviewed by HR and may be reimbursed only up to RM100.

### 3.3 Accommodations

Covers hotel rooms for business trips that need an overnight stay.

- The nightly cap depends on the employee's rank:

| Employee rank | Nightly cap (RM) |
|---|---|
| Rank 1 and 2 | 250 |
| Rank 3 and 4 | 400 |
| Rank 5 and above | 600 |

- Auto-approval amount: RM250 per night.
- Hotel cost above the nightly cap for the employee's rank is not reimbursed. The
  claim is approved for the capped amount only (a partial approval), and the
  employee pays the difference.
- The hotel folio must show the check-in date, the check-out date and the number
  of nights.
- Room service, minibar and laundry on the folio are not part of the room rate.

### 3.4 Equipment

Covers small work equipment such as keyboards, mice, headsets, cables and adapters.

- Auto-approval amount: RM300 per item.
- Equipment above RM300 needs a purchase request approved by HR before it is bought.
- Equipment stays company property.

## 4. Approval

1. HR reviews every claim that is above its category's auto-approval amount, or
   that Reclaim flags for review.
2. HR members may not approve their own claims.
3. Claims above RM1,000 need approval from two different HR members.

## 5. Overpayments

If an audit finds that a claim was overpaid, HR sends the employee a notice with
the amount and the reason. The employee has 5 days to dispute it. After that, the
amount is deducted from the employee's next claim, or the employee is invoiced.
